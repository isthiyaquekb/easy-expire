Delete Account & Associated Data

Application: Easy Expire (com.kambatha.easyexpire)
Developer: Isthiyaque Kambathagothi • Last updated: October 27, 2026

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users of Easy Expire have the right to request full and permanent deletion of their account credentials, user profile, inventory records, and all associated data.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once executed, your entire inventory catalog, batch numbers, alerts, and store profile are immediately purged from our servers and cannot be recovered.

How to Delete Your Account

You may delete your account directly inside the mobile application (recommended for immediate deletion) or submit an email request.

Option 1: In-App Immediate Deletion (Recommended)

You can execute an instant, self-service deletion directly inside the Easy Expire application without waiting for manual processing:

  1. 1 Open the Easy Expire app on your device and make sure you are signed in.
  2. 2 Tap the Settings icon on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. 3 Under the Account section, select Delete My Account.
  4. 4 If prompted, re-enter your password to authenticate your ownership of the account.
  5. 5 Review the warning modal and confirm by tapping Delete.

Result: The app immediately runs a cascading deletion script, purges all your data across cloud servers, cancels device alarms, erases local device storage, and signs you out.

Option 2: Email Deletion Request (Web / Offline Request)

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your mobile device, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

  • Send To: developer.isthiyaque@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Delete my account
  • Requirements: You must send the request directly from the registered email address associated with your Easy Expire account. Please include your Store / Business name in the message body so we can verify ownership.
To: developer.isthiyaque@gmail.com

Processing Time: Once verified, your account and all data will be permanently wiped from our database within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once completed.

What Exactly Gets Deleted

Based on our real application deletion architecture, when an account deletion is executed:

  • Inventory Catalog (Cloud Firestore): Every product document in the products collection linked to your account ID (user_id) is permanently deleted. This includes product names, batch numbers, barcodes, quantities, and expiry dates.
  • In-App Notifications: All records in your notification history subcollection (Users/{uid}/notifications) are deleted.
  • User Profile Document: Your profile document in Users/{uid} containing your store name, address, contact phone number, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device push tokens is permanently deleted.
  • Firebase Authentication Account: Your authentication record and login credentials in Firebase Auth are deleted, terminating your user identity.
  • Local Device Cache & Alarms: All scheduled local device reminder alarms are cancelled, notification deduplication tables are cleared, and local storage tokens (GetStorage / SharedPreferences) are erased on the device.

Data Retention Policy Post-Deletion

Zero Data Retained: We do not retain archival snapshots, shadow copies, or backup archives of deleted accounts. Once deletion completes, all personal and inventory data is completely purged.
