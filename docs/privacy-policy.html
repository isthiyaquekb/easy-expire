Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 • Application: Easy Expire (com.kambatha.easyexpire)
Overview: Easy Expire is developed by Isthiyaque Kambathagothi. This Privacy Policy explains exactly how we collect, store, process, and protect your information when using our mobile application. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we run third-party advertising or commercial tracking.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Easy Expire collects and stores only the data strictly necessary to provide user authentication, cloud inventory synchronization, and expiration date alerting.

A. Account and Profile Information

When you create an account or update your profile, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used as your unique account credential for login, authentication, and password reset workflows.
  • Store / Business Name: Used to customize your inventory space and display greeting banners.
  • Store Address: Optional business address field managed in your profile.
  • Store / Contact Phone Number: Optional contact number associated with your business profile.

B. Inventory and Product Data

To alert you before products expire, the app stores the following data in cloud storage associated with your user ID:

  • Product Name: Title or description of the product.
  • Batch Number: Manufacturing or stock lot identifier.
  • Expiry Date: The calendar date on which the item expires.
  • Barcode / SKU: Scanned or manually entered numeric or alphanumeric code.
  • Quantity: Number of units in stock.
  • Days Remaining: Calculated time delta between current date and expiration date.

C. Device Identifiers

  • FCM Registration Token: A unique device installation token generated by Firebase Cloud Messaging. This token is saved to your user record so our services can route timely expiration warnings and system notifications to your device.

D. Local Camera & Vision Processing (On-Device Only)

The app offers optical barcode scanning and text recognition (OCR) to detect expiry dates directly from product packaging. All visual analysis is processed exclusively in-memory on your device using on-device ML Kit models. No photographs, camera streams, or video frames are ever saved to disk, uploaded to cloud servers, or transmitted off your device.

E. Local Device Preferences

The following settings are stored solely in local device storage (via SharedPreferences / GetStorage) and are not synced to other users:

  • Lead alert threshold (e.g., 7 days, 15 days, 30 days before expiry)
  • Store opening digest time (e.g., 08:00 AM)
  • Urgent reminder frequency (e.g., every 2 hours or 4 hours)
  • Custom notification ringtone selection
  • Timestamps of dismissed notification banners (used to avoid spamming within 24 hours)
  • Local session authentication tokens

2. Purpose of Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authenticating your identity and preventing unauthorized access to your inventory.
  • Synchronizing your inventory data across sessions and authorized devices in real time.
  • Calculating expiry dates and delivering scheduled daily digest notifications and urgent reminders.
  • Facilitating fast optical barcode scanning and package date extraction.
  • Enabling direct customer support and responding to account deletion requests.

3. How Data is Stored and Protected

Your security is paramount:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application and backend services is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Cloud Storage: All account credentials and inventory records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication with strict user-level access rules ensuring only authenticated account owners can read or write their inventory.
  • No Unrelated Third-Party Access: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your inventory or personal information to data brokers, ad networks, or marketing agencies.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

Easy Expire relies on select enterprise backend services provided by Google LLC. These services process data solely on our behalf under strict confidentiality and privacy agreements:

Service Provider Purpose Privacy Policy

5. Device Permissions Requested

The app requests the following system permissions to provide core features:

Permission Platform Why It Is Needed
CAMERA Android & iOS Required to scan product barcodes and read expiry dates printed on product packaging. Frames are analyzed locally in-memory only.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android 13+ & iOS Required to display daily inventory expiration digests and urgent expiry reminders.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Android 12+ Required to trigger your morning store opening digest and time-critical expiry warnings at exact user-configured times.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Android Required to restore and reschedule active expiration alarms after your device is restarted.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Android 13+ Allows you to browse and select a custom local audio ringtone for your expiry alert notifications.
INTERNET / Network Android & iOS Required to sync inventory records with Cloud Firestore and authenticate login sessions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your profile and inventory records only for as long as your account remains active. You maintain total ownership and control over your data:

  • Immediate In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated inventory data at any time inside the app by navigating to Settings → Delete My Account.
  • Web Request: You can submit a deletion request via our public Account Deletion Page or by emailing developer.isthiyaque@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 30 days.
  • What Gets Deleted: Deleting your account triggers an irreversible cascade deletion of:
    1. All product records in the cloud inventory collection belonging to your user ID.
    2. All in-app notification subcollection records.
    3. Your user profile document (store name, address, phone, FCM token).
    4. Your authentication account in Firebase Auth.
    5. All locally stored preferences, cached sessions, and scheduled device alarms.
  • Zero Post-Deletion Retention: We do not retain backup copies or archived clones of deleted user inventory records.

7. User Rights

Under applicable data privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access all personal data and inventory items stored in your account.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information via the in-app Edit Profile and Edit Product screens.
  • Delete all personal data and store inventory immediately and permanently.
  • Withdraw consent for push notifications or camera access at any time through your device system settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Easy Expire is a specialized business and retail inventory tracking tool. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at developer.isthiyaque@gmail.com so we can delete the account.
